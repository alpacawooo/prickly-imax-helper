# Prickly IMAX Helper Official-Still Video Carousel Design

## Status and relationship to the earlier design

This design replaces only the static nine-slide carousel section of `2026-08-14-imax-reels-carousel-hook-design.md`. The approved hook, value proposition, CTA, product claims, privacy rules, and standalone Reel remain unchanged.

The new carousel is an eight-card mixed-motion Instagram carousel. Every card is exported as a short MP4, including cards whose primary visual is a still image. The benchmark account is used only for its persuasion sequence and video-carousel behavior. Its visual identity, copy, mascot, colors, and topic-specific layouts are not copied.

## Objective

Turn the existing carousel into a higher-retention sequence that:

1. touches the target viewer's Yongsan IMAX frustration on the cover;
2. converts frustration into recognition and curiosity;
3. demonstrates the real local Helper instead of merely describing it;
4. previews the actual Notion installation guide that a commenter will receive; and
5. ends with one comment keyword.

The narrative is:

`페르소나의 문제 → 공감 → 기존 선택지의 한계 → 해결책 → 실제 작동 증명 → 안전성 → 받을 자료 미리보기 → CTA`

## Benchmark elements to adopt

- One audience problem per cover, expressed in large type.
- One clear job per card rather than paragraphs of explanation.
- A numbered sequence that rewards continued swiping.
- Actual deliverables and outputs shown on screen before the CTA.
- A short screen recording of the resource sent after commenting.
- Motion inside carousel cards to increase dwell time: subtle zooms for stills and direct screen recordings for proof cards.
- A single comment keyword at the end.

## Benchmark elements not to copy

- The AI Freaks name, mascot, logo, character, character-sheet format, white-grid layout, pink/blue palette, typography, captions, or wording.
- The benchmark's specific topic, prompt-product framing, or screenshots.
- Any follower count, engagement count, account UI, or social proof belonging to the benchmark account.

Prickly retains its established near-black editorial layout, white heavy Korean typography, signal red accent, centered `prickly.ai` mark, restrained page count, and spacious final CTA.

## Eight-card sequence

All cards are 1080×1350 MP4 files, 30 fps, H.264, yuv420p, with a matching PNG cover frame. Critical text remains inside the central safe area. Sound is optional and the meaning must remain complete when muted.

### Card 1 — Persona pain / cover

- Duration: 6 seconds, seamless loop.
- Visual: official Trojan Horse beach still, darkened and slowly zoomed by no more than 3%.
- Copy: `용아맥 한 자리 보는데 30만 원?`
- Qualifier: `일부 리셀 게시물 사례`.
- Purpose: stop the exact viewer who wants Odyssey in Yongsan IMAX.

### Card 2 — Recognition

- Duration: 6 seconds.
- Visual: Antinous still with a dark scrim; three anonymized, paraphrased reaction cards enter one at a time.
- Copy: `보고 싶은 사람은 많은데 원하는 날짜는 이미 매진.`
- Reactions: repeated sellout, cancellation seats disappearing quickly, and giving up on Yongsan IMAX.
- Purpose: make the viewer feel that the post understands their current experience.

### Card 3 — Existing choices fail

- Duration: 7 seconds.
- Visual: Odysseus still with three sequential labels.
- Copy sequence: `새로고침` → `포기` → `비싼 리셀`.
- Closing line: `셋 다 내가 원한 해결은 아니었다.`
- Purpose: create the need for an alternative without over-explaining the product.

### Card 4 — Real setup proof

- Duration: 9 seconds.
- Visual: direct recording of the actual local Prickly IMAX Helper setup UI.
- Action: open the setup page, reveal dedicated Chrome login, and briefly scroll through movie, theater, format, time, party size, allowed rows, and seat preference.
- Copy: `설치 → 직접 로그인 → 원하는 조건 설정`.
- Privacy: use seeded demo values and no credentials, cookies, email address, voucher number, or live CGV page.
- Purpose: prove that the product is real and understandable to a non-developer.

### Card 5 — Real monitoring proof

- Duration: 8 seconds.
- Visual: redacted local status card with short number transitions.
- Show: `armed`, open dates, eligible shows, `match:null`, and errors `0` only when the current redacted diagnose supports those values.
- Copy: `지금 열린 날짜부터 새로 열릴 날짜까지.`
- Explanation: `match:null은 실패가 아니라 조건에 맞는 좌석을 기다리는 중.`
- Purpose: demonstrate the result without fabricating a booking or mobile ticket.

### Card 6 — Safety before speed

- Duration: 7 seconds.
- Visual: four safety cards appear sequentially.
- Show: duplicate booking block, required IMAX voucher count, zero remaining balance, and one final submission.
- Copy: `빠르기 전에 틀리지 않는 게 먼저.`
- Footer: `카드 결제 자동화 없음`.
- Purpose: answer the viewer's trust and risk objections.

### Card 7 — What the commenter receives

- Duration: 9 seconds.
- Visual: logged-out recording of the actual public Prickly Notion guide.
- Show: the guide title, macOS/Windows choice, installation command or download distinction, dedicated Chrome login, editable conditions, voucher explanation, update, and uninstall sections.
- Copy: `댓글을 남기면 이 설치 안내를 보내준다.`
- Privacy: hide browser bookmarks, account avatar, workspace navigation, private page titles, email addresses, and non-public Notion content. The public URL must work in a logged-out session before recording.
- Purpose: reduce uncertainty by previewing the real DM deliverable before asking for a comment.

### Card 8 — CTA

- Duration: 6 seconds, restrained loop.
- Visual: near-black background with a minimal red light sweep; no film still and no busy product screenshot.
- Copy: `댓글에 “아이맥스”라고 남기면 설치 방법을 보내줄게.`
- Supporting line: `Mac · Windows / 내 컴퓨터 · 내 CGV 계정 · 내 IMAX 관람권`.
- Purpose: give one clear action after the viewer has seen the problem, product, proof, and deliverable.

## Motion and visual system

- Motion is functional, not decorative. It reveals information, demonstrates operation, or prevents a still card from feeling inert.
- Still-image cards use a maximum 3% zoom, gentle two-axis drift, or restrained red light sweep. No rapid parallax or fake camera shake.
- Product and Notion cards use actual cursor/scroll behavior at readable speed. Do not accelerate so much that labels become illegible.
- Use one transition vocabulary: 150–250 ms opacity and position reveals, without flashy presets.
- Keep the top red rule, centered `prickly.ai`, and page count `01 / 08` through `08 / 08` consistent.
- Text must remain readable on a muted phone at normal feed size.

## Component and asset boundaries

- `official-stills/`: downloaded source stills plus source metadata; never overwritten by processed crops.
- `processed-stills/`: 1080×1350 crop-safe, desaturated, darkened versions used by Cards 1–3.
- `screen-recordings/`: sanitized local Helper and public Notion captures.
- `cards/`: one scene source and one final MP4/PNG pair per card.
- `contact-sheet/`: thumbnail QA across all eight cover frames.
- `package/`: publish-ready MP4s, PNG cover frames, caption/source notes, and checksums.

The renderer must accept a card number, duration, background asset, headline, supporting copy, and motion preset so typography and page chrome are not duplicated by hand.

## Data flow

1. Download official source stills and record their source URLs.
2. Produce non-destructive 1080×1350 crops with dark overlays and text-safe positioning.
3. Record the local setup UI offline with demo values.
4. Obtain a fresh redacted local status only through the existing diagnose command; do not browse CGV.
5. Verify the public Notion guide while logged out, then record only the public page.
6. Render each card to its own MP4 and PNG cover.
7. Run mechanical QA, contact-sheet review, source/claim review, and privacy review.
8. Package the final eight cards in posting order with checksums.

## Failure handling

- If a public Notion page requires login, contains private navigation, or exposes personal information, do not record it. Stop and fix or sanitize the public guide first.
- If the live diagnose contains identifiers or unexpected fields, do not use the raw screen. Render only the approved redacted subset.
- If an official still cannot be retrieved at sufficient quality, keep the existing generated theater background rather than upscale a social screenshot.
- If official-still reuse rights are needed for paid promotion and cannot be confirmed, replace the still with an original Odyssey-inspired asset before running the ad.
- Do not fabricate a ticket, seat match, voucher application, booking completion, engagement count, or Notion download success.

## Verification

### Mechanical

- Exactly eight MP4 cards and eight PNG cover frames.
- Each card is exactly 1080×1350, 30 fps, H.264, yuv420p.
- Durations match the card specification within 0.1 seconds.
- No missing source asset, silent rendering error, text overflow, or frame outside the safe zone.
- SHA-256 checksums generated for the final package.

### Human visual QA

- The cover problem is understandable within one second.
- Each card communicates one idea at thumbnail size.
- Official imagery supports the claim rather than competing with text.
- Product and Notion recordings remain readable on a phone.
- The final CTA preserves the restrained Prickly ending scale and negative space.

### Safety and claim QA

- `30만 원` is labeled as an example from some resale posts, not a universal price.
- No ticket guarantee, CGV endorsement, scalper-beating claim, or fabricated booking proof.
- No credentials, cookies, identifiers, voucher numbers, email addresses, private Notion pages, or user account UI.
- No CGV navigation, showtime click, party selection, seat selection, voucher action, or payment action occurs during production.
- The removed brand sentence `Prickly AI는 사람이 반복하던 일을 실제로 작동하는 자동화로 바꾼다.` does not reappear.

## Acceptance criteria

- The eight cards follow the approved problem-to-CTA persuasion sequence.
- At least Cards 4, 5, and 7 provide real, privacy-safe proof instead of generic mockups.
- Every card works without sound and earns its motion.
- The Prickly visual identity remains recognizable and does not imitate the benchmark account.
- The package is ready to upload as an Instagram video carousel, subject to the stated official-still rights boundary and the public Notion logged-out check.
