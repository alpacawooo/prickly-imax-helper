# Prickly IMAX Helper Reels and Carousel Hook Design

## Objective

Create Korean Instagram Reel and carousel drafts that begin with the current audience reaction to Odyssey Yongsan IMAX: repeated sellouts, people giving up after checking constantly, large waiting queues, and resale posts priced far above face value. The content must move quickly from that frustration to a practical alternative: letting the viewer's own computer wait for matching cancellation seats.

The account concept must be demonstrated through the product and outcome. Do not include an explanatory brand line such as `Prickly AI는 사람이 반복하던 일을 실제로 작동하는 자동화로 바꾼다.`

## Audience Insight

The supplied community screenshots repeatedly express four reactions:

- `아직 IMAX로 못 봤다` and `용아맥은 포기했다`.
- Desired dates are already sold out despite frequent CGV app checks.
- Yongsan IMAX demand and waiting queues make manual cancellation-seat hunting feel futile.
- Resale posts at several times face value create anger and resignation.

These screenshots are evidence of audience sentiment, not verified market-wide price data. Content may describe the supplied examples as resale posts, but must not present every listed price or queue count as a universal fact.

## Message Architecture

### Problem

People who want to watch Odyssey in Yongsan IMAX either keep refreshing, give up, or consider resale listings far above face value. Cancellation seats can disappear before a person notices them.

### Solution

Prickly IMAX Helper runs locally on the viewer's Mac or Windows PC and monitors current and newly opened booking dates using the viewer's own CGV login.

### How it helps

The viewer installs the Helper, logs in directly through dedicated Chrome, and chooses movie, theater, format, time, party size, rows, edge exclusion, and seat preference. The computer then waits for matching same-row consecutive seats. Existing-booking, voucher-count, zero-balance, and one-submit safeguards remain visible but concise.

### Value proposition

`하루 종일 새로고침하지 않아도, 내 조건에 맞는 취소표를 내 컴퓨터가 대신 기다려준다.`

### CTA

`댓글에 “아이맥스”라고 남기면 설치 방법을 보내줄게.`

## Primary Hook

Use a two-beat hook:

1. `용아맥 한 자리 보는데 30만 원?`
2. `그래서 누구나 자기 컴퓨터로 정가 취소표를 기다릴 수 있게 만들었다.`

The first line refers to the supplied resale-post example. Supporting caption copy must avoid implying that 300,000 won is the standard resale price.

## Reel Design

Target length: 25–30 seconds.

1. **0–3 seconds — Anger and recognition:** resale example, sellout reactions, and the primary hook.
2. **3–7 seconds — Problem definition:** constant refresh, immediate sellouts, and users giving up.
3. **7–11 seconds — Reveal:** show Prickly IMAX Helper as the alternative, without a brand-explainer sentence.
4. **11–18 seconds — Simple operation:** install, direct CGV login, and configurable booking conditions.
5. **18–23 seconds — Result:** local computer remains armed and waits for matching cancellation seats and future dates.
6. **23–27 seconds — Safety and honesty:** same-row seats, registered IMAX vouchers, zero remaining balance, one submission; no success guarantee.
7. **27–30 seconds — CTA:** comment keyword `아이맥스`.

Use screen recordings, redacted UI, kinetic type, and stylized seat diagrams. Do not present a simulated mobile ticket as a real completed booking.

## Carousel Design

Use nine slides so the story stays compact.

1. **Cover:** `용아맥 한 자리 보는데 30만 원?`
2. **Audience problem:** `보고 싶은 사람은 많은데, 원하는 날짜는 이미 전석 매진.`
3. **Failed behavior:** `하루 종일 새로고침하거나, 포기하거나, 비싼 리셀표를 보게 된다.`
4. **Reframe:** `그래서 사람 대신 컴퓨터가 취소표를 기다리게 했다.`
5. **Setup:** dedicated Chrome login plus editable movie, theater, format, time, seats, and rows.
6. **Monitoring:** current dates, newly opened dates, and matching consecutive seats.
7. **Safety:** duplicate prevention, exact IMAX voucher count, zero balance, and one submission.
8. **Value:** `암표를 사는 대신, 정가 취소표를 기다릴 수 있는 선택지.`
9. **CTA:** `댓글에 “아이맥스”라고 남기면 설치 방법을 보내줄게.`

## Tone and Claim Boundaries

- Sound direct, useful, and slightly indignant, not self-congratulatory.
- Lead with the viewer's frustration rather than a product feature list.
- Do not say the Helper defeats scalpers, guarantees tickets, eliminates resale, or is endorsed by CGV.
- Do not claim a real booking unless a verified mobile ticket exists.
- Do not show credentials, cookies, voucher numbers, email addresses, ticket identifiers, or private browser state.
- Make clear that the Helper runs on the user's own computer and uses the user's own account and registered IMAX vouchers.
- Keep the account concept implicit: the finished tool and its practical outcome are the proof.

## Draft Deliverables

- One 25–30 second Reel script with timecodes, on-screen copy, and shot direction.
- One nine-slide carousel copy deck with cover and body text.
- Five cover/hook variants for A/B testing.
- Reel caption, carousel caption, pinned comment, and comment-to-DM CTA.
- A final claim and privacy review checklist.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The problem is understood within the first two seconds.
- The product reveal follows the problem without a separate Prickly AI mission statement.
- A non-technical viewer can understand the three-step use flow.
- The value proposition is visible before the CTA.
- Exact resale examples are framed as supplied community examples, not universal pricing facts.
- Every product claim matches the released local runtime and its safety boundaries.
